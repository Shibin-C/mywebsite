The Old Man and the Wooden Box

Once upon a time, there lived an old man in a small village. He had a son who was very lazy and never wanted to work. The old man was worried about his son's future.

One day, the old man called his son and gave him a small wooden box.

“Take care of this box. It contains something valuable,” he said.

The son became curious and asked, “What is inside the box?”

The old man replied, “You will understand its value one day.”

The son kept the box safely but never opened it. After a few days, he became impatient and decided to open it. To his surprise, there was no gold, money, or jewellery inside. There was only a small piece of paper.

“The most valuable thing in life is not what you receive, but what you learn through your efforts.”

The son did not understand the message immediately. But he started thinking about his father's words.

The next morning, he decided to find a job. Initially, he struggled because he had no experience. Sometimes he made mistakes, and sometimes he felt disappointed. But he did not give up.

After several months, he became more responsible and confident. He finally understood what his father wanted to teach him.

When he met his father, he said, “Now I understand the real value of the box. It taught me that nothing valuable comes without effort.”

The old man smiled and said, “Exactly. Money can be lost, but the knowledge and experience you gain through hard work will always remain with you.”

Moral of the Story: Hard work and experience are more valuable than temporary wealth.
